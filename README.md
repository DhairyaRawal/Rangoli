# Reminiscing Rangoli

Paint a Diwali rangoli on a courtyard floor, right in your browser, on phone or PC.

**Play:** https://dhairyarawal.github.io/rangoli/

## How to play

1. Pick a tool: **Powder**, **Dots**, **Petals**, **Leaves** or **Diya**.
2. Choose a colour and how many times your design repeats around the centre (1 to 12).
3. Drag to pour powder, or tap to place dots, petals, Ashoka leaves and diyas (up to 15).
4. Every move becomes a layer. Select a layer to change its colour, size, repeat or turn, or to duplicate, reorder, hide or delete it.
5. Press **Done** to light the diyas, then download your rangoli as a PNG.

## Run locally

It's a single HTML file with no build step. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
